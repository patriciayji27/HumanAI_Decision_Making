# Topic Map: Research Streams in Human-Agent Decision Making

This archive organizes research into eight interconnected streams. Each stream traces a research direction from its foundations through key developments to the current frontier.

## How the Streams Connect

The streams are not independent — they form a network. Here is the high-level logic:

**Inner core (the decision itself):**
- **Utility & Decision Quality** (Stream 3) is the normative and descriptive center: what should rational choice look like, and how does it actually look?
- **Risk & Ambiguity** (Stream 5) extends this to different *types* of uncertainty.

**Input side (what information reaches the decision maker, and how):**
- **Belief Elicitation** (Stream 1) asks how we extract beliefs — the *input* to decisions.
- **Uncertainty Communication** (Stream 2) asks how uncertainty information is *presented*.
- **Sampling & Representation** (Stream 7) asks how people *learn* about uncertainty from experience and data.

**Cognitive constraints (what the mind does with that information):**
- **Attention, Memory & Salience** (Stream 4) governs what gets noticed and remembered.
- **Temporal Dynamics** (Stream 6) governs how time structure (feedback, evaluation frequency) shapes processing.

**The AI partner:**
- **Agents as Advisors** (Stream 8) sits at the intersection of all other streams — AI agents elicit beliefs (Stream 1), communicate uncertainty (Stream 2), may substitute for or complement human judgment (Stream 3), compete for attention (Stream 4), and operate under various types of uncertainty (Stream 5).

## Reading Order Suggestions

- **If you're new to the area:** Start with Stream 3 (Utility & Decision Quality) — it provides the normative backbone, and Prospect Theory connects to almost everything else.
- **If you're building decision interfaces:** Start with Stream 2 (Uncertainty Communication) → Stream 4 (Attention & Salience) → Stream 8 (Agents as Advisors).
- **If you're interested in AI-human complementarity:** Start with Stream 8 → Stream 1 (Belief Elicitation) → Stream 3.
- **If you're studying experience-based learning:** Start with Stream 7 (Sampling) → Stream 6 (Temporal Dynamics) → Stream 5 (Risk & Ambiguity).
