Explanations Can Reduce Overreliance on AI Systems

Vasconcelos, Jörke et al. · , 2023

AI Agents

The specific finding

Explanations reduce overreliance only when evaluation effort is low relative to stakes:

ConditionOverreliance reduction
Easy explanation + high stakes12-20 pp
Hard explanation + high stakesNot significant
Easy explanation + low stakesSmall or absent

This reconciles conflicting prior work: Bansal et al. (2021) and Buçinca et al. (2021) used tasks where explanation evaluation was expensive relative to stakes — placing users in the region where engaging is irrational. Design implication: for any explanation, estimate evaluation time and compare to decision stakes. If cost exceeds stakes, the explanation anchors rather than informs.
