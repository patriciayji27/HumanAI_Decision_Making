Ambiguity Aversion, Robustness, and the Variational Representation of Preferences

Maccheroni, Marinacci & Rustichini · Econometrica, 2006

Risk & Ambiguity

The specific contribution

Variational preferences (VP) unify several previously separate models of decision under ambiguity under a single axiomatic framework. The key representation: a decision maker's utility from an act f equals minp{ Ep[u(f)] + c(p) }, where c(p) is a cost function penalizing deviations from a reference prior. By choosing different cost functions, VP recovers max-min EU (Gilboa-Schmeidler), multiplier preferences (Hansen-Sargent), and mean-variance preferences as special cases.

Qualifications

Highly technical axiomatic paper. The empirical identification problem is substantial — the cost function c(p) has many possible forms, and behavioral data typically cannot distinguish between competing specifications without strong parametric restrictions. Primarily used in macroeconomics and financial economics for robust decision making and asset pricing.
