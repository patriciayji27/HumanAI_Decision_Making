Salience Theory of Choice Under Risk

Bordalo, Gennaioli & Shleifer · , 2012

Decision Quality Attention & Salience

The specific idea

People don't distort probabilities. They overweight whichever payoff states grab attention. A state is salient when the difference between options' payoffs is large relative to their average.

If the mechanism is...InterventionScalability
Probability weighting (PT)Training / debiasingExpensive, fragile, per-user
Salience / attention (this paper)Redesign the displayCheap, permanent, all users

Produces the same predictions as PT for Allais-type problems but through attention, not distortion. Empirical track record is shorter than PT. Divergent predictions are hard to test outside the lab.
