Meta-analysis of Empirical Estimates of Loss Aversion

Brown, Imai, Vieider & Camerer · , 2024

Decision Quality

The specific contribution

Largest loss aversion meta-analysis: 607 estimates from 150 articles. Mean λ = 1.96 [1.82, 2.10]. Substantial between-study heterogeneity; few study characteristics explain the variation.

Contested by Walasek et al. (2024): λ≈1.31 from individual re-fitting. And by Yechiam et al. (2025): λ≈1.07 when stimulus asymmetry and ordering are controlled. The debate is active and unresolved — the "true" population λ may depend on which methodological choices you consider legitimate.
