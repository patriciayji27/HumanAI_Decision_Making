Numeric, Verbal, and Visual Formats of Conveying Health Risks

Lipkus · Medical Decision Making, 2007

Uncertainty Communication

The specific contribution

Review of 30+ studies on formats for communicating health risk probabilities. The most practically useful review paper for uncertainty communication format selection: systematically compares numeric (percentages, frequencies), verbal (likely/unlikely/rare), and visual (bar charts, icon arrays, pie charts) formats across comprehension, risk perception, and behavior outcomes.

Key findings by format

Bar chartsGoodCan mislead on relative risk magnitudeComparative risk between options

No single format dominates across all audiences and tasks. The review's most actionable finding: icon arrays are the most robust format for low-numeracy populations, which represent 30–50% of most adult samples.

Qualifications

Health communication context; generalization to AI confidence displays requires attention to domain differences (health risk is usually binary; AI uncertainty is often continuous or multiclass). Review covers comprehension primarily; fewer studies address behavioral outcomes from format differences.
