The Misinformation Effect of Explanations in Human-AI Collaboration

Schemmer, Hemmer, Kuehl & Vössing · , 2025

AI Agents

The specific finding

Damage from wrong explanations is ~3× the benefit from correct ones:

AI predictionExplanationEffect on accuracy
CorrectCorrect+5 pp
IncorrectIncorrect−15 pp
IncorrectCorrectPartial recovery

N=370 + N=384, architectural style classification. Wrong explanations didn't just mislead — they made participants more confident in wrong answers.

If the 3:1 ratio holds, explanation features have negative expected value when accuracy < ~75%. Explanation accuracy — not just model accuracy — should be a shipping criterion. Tested in one domain; needs replication.
