How to Improve Bayesian Reasoning Without Instruction: Frequency Formats

Gigerenzer & Hoffrage · , 1995

Uncertainty Communication Belief Elicitation

The specific finding

Same information, different format, dramatically different performance:

Probability format

"The probability of breast cancer is 1%. If a woman has breast cancer, the probability of a positive mammogram is 80%. If not, probability of positive is 9.6%. She tests positive. What is the probability she has cancer?"

Correct: 16%

Natural frequency format

"10 of 1,000 women have breast cancer. Of these 10, 8 test positive. Of the remaining 990, 95 also test positive. How many women who test positive actually have cancer?"

Correct: 46%

Answer is ~7.8% in both cases. Frequencies preserve the reference class structure ("8 out of 103 positives"), making set-inclusion visible without computation. Benefit is specific to Bayesian integration tasks; smaller for simple probability judgments (Brase, 2008). Frequencies must be "natural" (preserving sampling tree).
