When Combinations of Humans and AI Are Useful

Vaccaro, Almaatouq & Malone · , 2024

AI Agents

The specific finding

Meta-analysis of 106 studies. Complementarity (team > max(human, AI)) achieved in a minority of conditions. The modal outcome: AI alone ≈ human-AI team, meaning the human often added noise.

ModeratorComplementarity more likelyLess likely
AI accuracyModerate (room for human override)Very high (override usually hurts) or very low
Task difficultyHigh (neither reliable alone)Low (AI sufficient; human adds noise)

Publication bias likely inflates the apparent success rate — studies showing complementarity failure are less publishable.
