To Trust or to Think: Cognitive Forcing Functions Reduce Overreliance

Buçinca, Malaya & Gajos · , 2021

AI Agents Attention & Salience

The specific finding

Cognitive forcing (committing to a prediction before seeing AI) reduced agreement with incorrect AI from 69% to 52%. LIME explanations without forcing: no effect — statistically indistinguishable from baseline.

Cost: ~50% more time per decision. Lower satisfaction. Feasible for high-stakes, low-frequency decisions; impractical for routine automation.

The broader point: the interaction protocol (when the user sees the AI output) is a separate and sometimes more effective design lever than the AI output itself (accuracy, calibration, explanation quality).
