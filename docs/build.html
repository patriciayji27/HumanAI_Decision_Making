Paper digestion criteria

A paper is included if it meets at least one of these conditions:

Paradigm or measure. Introduced a paradigm, method, or measure still in active use.
Empirical shift. Produced a finding that changed design practice or theoretical consensus.
Meta-analysis or review. Settled or reopened a debate by aggregating existing evidence.
Frontier (2023-2026). Shifts the frontier of human-AI collaboration with new evidence or frameworks.

Excluded: purely technical ML papers with no human-decision link, or replications that add no new qualification.

Paper verdict format

Each paper gets two fields:
Verdict. The specific contribution and why it matters for the field.
Qualification. Later work that weakened, qualified, or contested the finding. Boundary conditions. Heterogeneity.

Epistemic taxonomy (2-axis)

AxisValueMeaning
EvidenceStrongRobust, widely replicated, survives meta-analytic scrutiny
MixedReplicated in some contexts, qualified in others, or competing interpretations
WeakSmall evidence base, limited replication, or too new to assess
StatusCanonicalPart of the field's working knowledge
DebatedActive disagreement or important qualifications
EmergingRecent, not yet integrated into mainstream

These axes are independent. A paper can be strong evidence and debated status (robust effect, contested mechanism).
